# Installer translation and runtime

ChineseTraditional.isl is the unchanged Traditional Chinese translation from
jrsoftware/issrc tag is-6_7_1, Files/Languages/Unofficial/ChineseTraditional.isl.
Its original translator credits are preserved. Inno Setup copyright and terms
are preserved in inno-LICENSE.txt from the same upstream tag.
Upstream: https://github.com/jrsoftware/issrc/tree/is-6_7_1
The translation is vendored because Windows build installations do not always
include unofficial language files. It is not project-authored MIT material.
